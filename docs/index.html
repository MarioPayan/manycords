Collections / sources / conversations

01 / Choose

Your channels, together.

Build collections across servers. Only channels included in your collections are captured. The initial “All” collection includes every server channel your tab receives; remove it to narrow your selection.

02 / Follow

Know where it came from.

Each channel gets its own coloured lane, so you always see which server and channel a message comes from. Search recent captured messages, filter by source and mark a collection as read here without changing its read state in Discord.

03 / Keep local

A reader on your device.

Messages stay in the browser’s extension storage. No uploads to us, no analytics and no additional account. Avatar, server icon and thumbnail images load from Discord’s CDN.

Get started

Keep your tab open.
Give your reading some space.

  1. Install the extension for your browser.
  2. Open or reload your own Discord browser tab.
  3. Open the reader from the toolbar and choose your collections.

New activity appears while that tab is open. If capture had a gap, the reader shows it and opens the channel at that moment; scroll up there and the missing messages are saved. ManyCords never navigates or scrolls for you. Direct messages are not saved.
